Rwanda Vision 2050
===

Introduction
Rwanda Vision 2050 sets a new path for the country's development, building on the success of Vision 2020. It aims to achieve high standards of living for all Rwandans.

Pillars of Vision 2050
1. Human Development: Ensuring quality education, healthcare using technology, and universal access to basic services.
2. Competitiveness and Integration: Focusing on export-led growth, regional integration, and key infrastructure.
3. Agriculture for Wealth Creation: Moving from subsistence to market-oriented agriculture.
4. Urbanization and Agglomeration: Developing secondary cities to support sustainable growth.
5. Accountable and Capable State Institutions: Maintaining peace, security, and justice.

Economic Target
The goal is to achieve upper-middle-income status by 2035 and high-income status by 2050. This requires an average annual growth rate of over 10%.

Key Drivers
- Innovation and technology adoption.
- Private sector-led development.
- Human capital development.
